Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Orders can be cancelled before dispatch or once the shipment has been booked as per policy.

Before dispatch

Orders cancelled before dispatch are generally refunded in full unless a partial processing fee applies.

After dispatch

Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. In that case, you may need to use the return policy after delivery.
