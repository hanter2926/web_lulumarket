Refund Policy

We aim to provide a smooth shopping experience. Refunds are processed according to the item condition and order status.

  • Orders cancelled before dispatch may be eligible for full refund.
  • Items returned after use may be subject to inspection.

Processing

Refunds are generally issued within 5–7 business days after approval and confirmation from our team.

How to request

Contact our support team with your order number and issue details for faster processing.
